Bulb and location validation audit

Reviewed November 8, 2026. This is a desk audit of every pre-audit inventory row and all marker/region classes. It is not a physical certification of the installed machine.

83 original rows reviewed. Retained 61 drawing-supported circuit/feature references with 63 retraced feature markers. Withdrew 17 guessed table-flasher points, 6 guessed backbox dots, 4 GI regions, and all 21 photo overlays. L52 and L84 no longer receive invented playfield socket points.

What changed

  • L52: two upper skull lamps. Assembly p. 2-27 and BOM specify #555 wedge, while the lamp chart says #44. The app now requires socket inspection and withholds a definite replacement recommendation.
  • L17 Mouth 2 and L51 Eyes Lower are playfield features, not the upper skull assembly. Their LED guidance was corrected.
  • GI output/function labels disagree between pp. 2-38 and 2-39. The app uses aggregate backbox/playfield GI records without invented routing or regions. Mixed playfield bulb families require inspection.
  • Backbox BOM: 37 #555 plus 6 #906 bulbs, 43 total. This validates an assembly total, not per-socket locations or circuit membership.
  • Factory drawings can contain feature symbols and circuit callouts. Neither is automatically an exact bulb-holder coordinate or a physical bulb count.

Limits of validation

  • 61 circuit references have retraced manual feature markers (63 drawn positions). These are feature/insert locations, not inspected socket centers.
  • L52 is documented as two lamps within a skull assembly, with a #555/#44 source conflict. No playfield dot.
  • L84 is a cabinet button with no verified map coordinate.
  • All flasher and GI socket coordinates are withheld. Quantities printed in a table or BOM do not validate individual locations.
  • Actual machine modifications, every installed bulb, and wire-to-socket mapping require inspection/testing of the machine.

Sources

  • Williams original operations manual
  • Reproduced T2 assembly BOM
  • Pinball Life skull-eye socket variant documentation

Excluded or unresolved references

  • L18: Not Used on p. 2-35; no bulb or marker added.
  • S28: Drop-target coil AE-26-1200 in p. 2-38 is not a flasher bulb, despite conflicting p. 2-39 wording; no lamp added.
  • Photo P1-P21: All 21 overlays removed. The photo is a visual reference and cannot validate factory IDs, families, circuit membership or a complete inventory.
  • GI-04: p. 2-38 says Not Used; p. 2-39 says CPU String. No fitted/absent claim or socket location is assigned from these conflicting labels.
