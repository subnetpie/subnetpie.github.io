Williams Terminator 2: Judgment Day · Lamp and circuit reference

Lighting types ⓘ

Controlled lamps · bulb base must match the installed holder

The game switches these lamps to communicate rules, awards and shot progress. Most sit behind inserts; T2 also controls its skull features and start button. A lamp blinking during play is not necessarily a flasher. For these 6.3V lamp-matrix positions, use non-ghosting LEDs to avoid a faint glow while off.

GI · general illumination · #555 in this inventory

GI lights the playfield and backglass artwork so you can see the game. T2’s top and bottom insert-board GI strings are in the backbox, in addition to the six flashers. It is normally on, though the machine can dim or switch GI strings for effects. Use diffused 6.3V LEDs for an even wash. This locator shades schematic GI regions; it does not assign individual GI socket positions or totals.

Flashers · #89 / #906

These are brief, bright effect lamps triggered for gameplay events. Replacements are sold as 12V pinball flasher LEDs; T2’s manual labels the pulsed flasher supply 20V. Choose replacements intended for WPC #89/#906 flasher sockets, not generic 12V lighting. #89 uses a bayonet base; #906 uses a wedge base. T2 has playfield and backbox flasher locations. Their map positions are approximate.

Bulb number identifies the electrical/base family; role and location determine the LED style. The lamp chart and parts assemblies can disagree. Upper skull eyes are a documented #555/#44 conflict; inspect the installed socket.

Sources: Williams T2 manual as represented in the locator; Comet: bulb families; Tilt Graphics: lighting roles.

LED choices & kits ⓘ

Recommendation for circuit selection

Audit correction: upper-eye sockets and playfield GI families require inspection. Product recommendations do not validate socket positions or override the installed holder.

Balanced recommendation

Use non-ghosting LEDs for controlled lamps, frosted LEDs for GI and exposed features, and dedicated 12V flashers with brightness chosen for the location. Match LED color to the existing lens; warm white is our preference for preserving the original GI appearance. Cool white is a crisper alternative.

This is our synthesis of supplier guidance, not an independent product test or a vote across owners. Comet recommends the common 1SMD non-ghosting / frosted 2SMD combination. Tilt Graphics also specifies non-ghosting inserts and offers a frosted kit. Sources checked November 8, 2026.

Three T2 conversion kits

Our default complete-kit choice

Comet · Terminator 2 kit

Explicit non-ghosting inserts, cool-white GI and pop-bumper discs. Optional ice-blue GI is included; strips and spotlights are excluded. Select the standard non-OCD version for a straightforward bulb conversion. The OCD version uses different insert bulbs plus a controller board; GI OCD is separate.

Tradeoff: stock GI is cool white. For our softer warm-white preference, ask about substitutions or buy the GI bulbs separately.

Tilt Graphics · premium non-ghosting T2 kit

Color-matched controlled lamps with a choice of frosted or super-bright styles. Lists controlled lamps, playfield GI, flashers and backbox GI as its four kit sections.

Best fit: choose frosted for a less harsh presentation. Super-bright is the stronger alternative. Check the included GI color before ordering.

Marco · T2 kit 05-1779

Lists flashers, both GI areas and switched feature lamps. Marco describes its color and size mix as based on owner consensus.

Tradeoff: the listing does not explicitly promise non-ghosting insert LEDs. Confirm that specification and the GI color with Marco before choosing it over an explicitly non-ghosting kit.

For a tailored result, buy individual non-ghosting inserts, warm-white frosted GI and location-appropriate flashers. A kit saves sorting time; it does not make maximum brightness the best choice everywhere.

Fit, ghosting and dimming

Match the original socket, voltage and available clearance. A 6.3V LED and a 12V flasher are different parts even when a replacement wedge physically fits. Inspect angled sockets before choosing a flex-head LED.

Non-ghosting bulbs address unwanted off-state glow; they do not recreate incandescent fade timing. If preserving fades matters, consider the compatible insert LED OCD option. GI control is a separate issue. Power off before changing bulbs.
